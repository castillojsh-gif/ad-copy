"Before I walk you through everything, I want to make sure I have a clear picture — based on what you have shared with me, it sounds like [summarize their pain in their words]. Is that fair?"

[They confirm — now pitch with full confidence that you know their situation]

"So [Name], based on everything you have shared with me, I am very confident we can help you. Let me walk you through how we actually do this — and I want you to stop me at any point if you have questions."

PILLAR 1 — THE FOUNDATION AUDIT
"The first thing we do before we build anything — or before we take on a single one of your clients — is get inside your GoHighLevel account and audit it completely. Every tag, every pipeline, every workflow, every automation.

The reason we do this first is because we have seen what happens when you skip it. We had a client come to us after another agency had built directly on top of a messy account. Within 60 days, automations were conflicting with each other and leads were falling through the cracks. We spent the first week just cleaning before we could build anything.

So we clean the foundation first. Every time. No exceptions. Because everything we build after this — and every client we onboard for you — is only as good as what it is built on."

[Pause] "Any questions on that or does that make sense?"

PILLAR 2 — THE ONBOARDING ENGINE
"Once the foundation is clean, we build your onboarding system. What this means is that when a new client signs, the entire experience runs automatically — contract fires, payment triggers the welcome sequence, calendar invite goes out, your team gets notified. Your client feels taken care of from minute one and you are not manually doing any of it.

Most agency owners we work with are spending 3 to 4 hours manually onboarding every single new client — differently every time. One of our clients told me she lost a client not because her service was bad but because the first impression was so chaotic the client did not trust her. We fixed that.

And once this is built and clean, we take it a step further. Every new client you hand us — we guarantee they are fully onboarded and live within 7 business days. That is our promise. Or we keep working until they are, at no additional charge."

[Pause] "Tracking with me so far? Does this sound like what you have been looking for?"

PILLAR 3 — THE FULFILLMENT MACHINE
"The third piece is where we build the entire fulfillment layer — and then we run it for you. Snapshot install, dedicated email domain, calendar connections, SMS and email activation, landing page live. Every new client you hand us goes through the same professional setup process, delivered by our team, in 7 business days.

But here is where we go further than anyone else. We build in the details that most people skip — professional email signatures for you and your entire team, your logo on every email, your favicon in the browser, your brand colors throughout. The things that make your agency look and feel like a premium company.

We have clients whose own clients comment on how professional and put-together everything looks after we build. One told me her client said 'I do not know what you changed but your business feels completely different now.' She had not changed her service. She had changed how it looked and felt. That is what we build — and then we keep running it for you every single month."

[Pause] "How are you feeling about the process so far?"

TEMPERATURE CHECK
"So overall — how are you feeling about what I have walked you through? Does this feel like what you have been looking for?"

If they are bought in → move to delivery.
If they seem uncertain:
"What part are you not 100% sure about? I want to make sure this is the right fit before we go any further."
