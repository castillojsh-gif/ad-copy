WHY THIS OFFER:
Explain the logic behind this offer and how you arrived at this structure.
"Mains Marketing is a white label GoHighLevel fulfillment partner for B2B agency owners. Our ideal client is an established agency — doing $20k–$100k/month — that uses GHL to deliver for their clients and is manually setting up every new client themselves or managing an offshore team that lacks accountability and critical thinking on edge cases.

The problem is not that they lack GHL knowledge. The problem is that their fulfillment process is inconsistent, founder-dependent, and breaks at volume. Every new client they onboard is a manual project. They cannot scale because the bottleneck is the setup process itself.

The 7-day fulfillment guarantee works because we control the deliverable completely. We are not promising marketing results that depend on close rates, ad spend, or their offer. We are promising a clean, professional client setup — a repeatable operational outcome that our team executes the same way every time.

This guarantee is only possible because of The Foundation Month. Before we touch a single client account, we spend 30 days auditing their GHL, overhauling their onboarding system, and building the internal documentation that lets our team execute without guessing. By the time we go live, we already know their system inside and out.

At $3,500 for The Foundation Month and $1,500/month retainer plus $500 per client, the math works for any agency onboarding 2 or more clients per month. Most of our target clients are onboarding 4–12 new clients monthly — making this a straightforward ROI conversation, not a cost conversation."
