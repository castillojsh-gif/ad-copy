# HOOK BANKS (Alen Sultanic)

Assign ONE structure per asset, no repeats within a package. Adapt phrasing wherever a bank line collides
with the kill list in CLAUDE.md ("But here's the truth," "Most people," two-beat contrasts) — keep the
structure, not the banned construction.

## 0. "Why haven't" identity hooks

Template: **Why haven't [identity] with [problem] been told these facts?**
Examples: first-time dog owners with anxious rescue puppies / men over 40 with low testosterone /
homeowners with foundation cracks / small landlords with problem tenants / freelancers with inconsistent
income / retirees with shrinking 401(k)s / truck drivers with chronic back pain / divorced dads with
custody battles / roofing contractors riding the feast-or-famine rollercoaster.

## 1. Direct payoff hooks
- "In the next [X minutes], I'm going to show you how to [OUTCOME] without [PAIN]."
- "Once you know how to [MECHANISM], you'll be able to [OUTCOME] so you can [DEEPER DESIRE]."
- "First you'll [STEP 1], then once you have [STEP 2], you'll be able to [OUTCOME]."
- "The reason this works comes down to [ONE MECHANISM]." / "The way it works is [SIMPLE MECHANISM]."
- Variants: "Hand me your next [30/90] days." / "Give us 90 days." / "Picture 90 days from now."

## 2. "Two types of people" hooks
- "There are only two types of [AVATAR]: the ones who [WIN] and the ones who watch them win."
- "Some [AVATAR] get [RESULT] easily. Others work harder and still get nowhere."
- "The people crushing it aren't doing [COMMON TACTIC]. They're doing [HIDDEN THING]."
- "For every [AVATAR] getting [RESULT], thousands are stuck with [PAIN]."

## 3. "How come?" hooks
- "How come your [BUSINESS] is still stuck?" / "How come you still have to [DAILY PAIN]?"
- "How come others are doing this easily while you can barely [PAINFUL REALITY]?"
- "How come you're doing everything right but still not getting [RESULT]?"
- "How come the people with less [SKILL/RESOURCES] are getting better results than you?"

## 4. Market-shift hooks
- "[X] used to be simple... but then the world changed."
- "A few years ago, [OLD STRATEGY] worked. Today, it leaves you stuck."
- "The thing that got you here is now the thing holding you back."
- "It's no longer about [OLD GAME]. It's about [NEW GAME]."
- "Every year, more [COMPETITORS] enter the market, which means [PAINFUL CONSEQUENCE]."
- "If you don't stand out, you don't get the best [CLIENTS/PATIENTS/BUYERS]."

## 5. Anti-guru / anti-common-advice hooks
- "Everyone tells you to [COMMON ADVICE]. But if that worked, wouldn't you already have [RESULT]?"
- "The advice you're following is the reason you're stuck."
- "The real problem isn't [SURFACE PROBLEM]. It's [DEEPER MECHANISM]."
- "You don't need more [OLD THING]. You need [MISSING PIECE]."
- "It has nothing to do with [COMMON BLAME]. It has everything to do with [HIDDEN CAUSE]."
- "If [COMMON METHOD] still worked, the gurus would be using it, not selling it."

## 6. Diagnostic hooks
- "If you're [AVATAR] and you've noticed [SYMPTOM 1] and [SYMPTOM 2], then chances are [DIAGNOSIS]."
- "If you've had [PROBLEM] for more than [TIMEFRAME], this is what's really happening."
- "You think the issue is [SURFACE CAUSE], but it's actually [ROOT CAUSE]."
- "You're not bad at [THING]. You've just been trained to do it the wrong way."
- "The reason [SYMPTOM] keeps coming back is because [OLD SOLUTIONS] never address [ROOT]."
- "Mistake #1 costs you [LOSS]. Mistake #2 wastes [RESOURCE]. Mistake #3 prevents [OUTCOME]."
- (Compliance-sensitive for health/medical claims.)

## 7. Secret / hidden mechanism hooks
- "There's a well-kept secret in [NICHE] only a few [AVATARS] know."
- "The few who know this secret get to [WIN], while everyone else watches."
- "There's a little-known third way to [OUTCOME]."
- "What nobody notices are the small moves that create the big wins."
- "Not all [LEADS/CLIENTS/BUYERS] are created equal."

## 8. Painful status contrast hooks
- "You watched everyone else [WIN] while you [PAINFUL REALITY]."
- "They're not smarter than you. They just know how to [MECHANISM]."
- "You're working harder than people getting better results."
- "The people you think are ahead of you aren't better. They have a system you don't."
- "It's a shame [LESS QUALIFIED PEOPLE] are getting [RESULT] while you're still [PAIN]."
  (Aggressive — prints in insecure markets, destroys trust in warm/sophisticated ones. Use carefully.)

## 9. "What if?" hooks
- "What if you and I could trade places?"
- "What if there was an easy, proven path instead of the hard path?"
- "What if you could [OUTCOME] without [SACRIFICE]?"
- "What if you had someone copy and paste [PROVEN SYSTEM] into your business?"
- "What if the opposite of what you've been taught is the answer?"

## 10. Refresh / don't-start-over hooks (strong B2B)
- "You don't need new [ASSET]. You need to refresh what already worked."
- "It's like getting an oil change instead of buying a new car."
- "Would you rather improve what's already proven or risk starting over?"
- "You're not broken. Your system just needs [SPECIFIC FIX]."

## 11. Email / lead-gen hooks
- "Whose emails do you actually read?"
- "Most people optimize for opens and clicks. We optimize for buyers." (adapt "most people")
- "Your leads aren't worthless — you're just not extracting the buyers." (adapt the dash)
- "You already have [X] new clients in your business and don't even know it."
- "A dead list isn't dead. It's just been trained not to care."
- "The money is hiding in the people who didn't buy yet."

## 12. Fear / unknown consequence hooks
- "What you don't know about [PROBLEM] can cost you [LOSS]."
- "If you don't handle [ISSUE] now, you're headed for [CONSEQUENCE]."
- "And that's how people end up with [PAINFUL RESULT] after doing everything right."

## 13. Proof / authority hooks
- "After managing [X], running [Y] campaigns, and completing [Z] tests, this is the pattern I keep seeing."
- "After working with [X] [AVATAR], one pattern shows up every time."
- "The reason I know this works is because [PROOF]."
- "I know because I used to be in your exact position."

## 14. Anti-agency / anti-service-provider hooks
- (USE WITH CARE — house rule: no agency-blame unless it's the client's genuine wedge.)
- "Instead of dealing with [AGENCY PAINS], what if you had someone who [BETTER OFFER]?"
- "We don't just build [ASSET]. We put our money behind it."

## 15. Visual / story contrast hooks
- "Meet [STRUGGLING PERSONA]. He does everything right and still gets no result. Now meet [WINNING PERSONA]."
- "Have you ever looked in the mirror, then seen a photo of yourself and barely recognized the person?"
- "How can you fix what's wrong when you can't even see it?"

## 16. From-to hooks
- "You go from [DREAM] to [NIGHTMARE] when [TRIGGER EVENT] happens."
- "You go from [SMALL SCALE] to [BIG SCALE], and that's where everything breaks."
- "By the end of this video, you'll know how to go from [CURRENT] to [DESIRED]."

## 17. Strange fact hooks
- "The first [SMALL PART] creates [BIG PERCENTAGE] of the result."
- "The 10% of [ACTIVITY] that delivers 90% of [RESULT] is [MECHANISM]."
- "Most people obsess over [BIG THING], but the result comes from [SMALL THING]." (adapt "most people")

## 18. Survival / world-changing hooks
- (Powerful but conspiratorial-fast. Only for survival/preparedness or strong fear-based markets.)
- "Take a look around. The world is changing."
- "This forgotten method goes back to [HISTORICAL MOMENT]."
