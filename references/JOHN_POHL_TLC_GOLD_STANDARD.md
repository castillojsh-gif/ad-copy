# GOLD STANDARD REFERENCE — John Pohl / The TLC System (Med Spas)
*Copy Chief's demonstration of the no-offer-led-openers rule (2026-07). Study before writing any package.*

## PATTERNS TO COPY (extracted)

1. **Guarantee placement:** mid-to-late in every asset, arriving only after the prospect is bought into
   the solution. Never the opener (Ultra Short may still lead with it per Franbeau, but even this
   reference opens its Ultra Short with the tribal-truth line and drops the guarantee after the value line).
2. **Guarantee weaving:** the risk-reversal clause stays verbatim everywhere ("or we keep working at no
   cost until you get there") while the promise half integrates into the sentence's own grammar:
   - "That's what we put in writing. 30 new patients in 90 days, or..."
   - "Give us 90 days... and 30 of them walk through your doors, or..."
   - "...which is exactly why we can guarantee 30 new ones in 90 days, or..."
   - "...we back that with a promise. 30 new patients in 90 days, or..."
   - "...which is how we can promise 30 new patients in 90 days, or..."
   Rotate these framing devices; never restate the spine identically as a standalone line in every asset.
3. **Long-form headers are custom, mechanism-derived** (THE TARGETING / THE MESSAGING / THE CARE),
   not the fixed STRATEGY/WHY/DIFFERENT set. Derive headers from the client's actual pillars.
4. **Sanctioned promise-opener** for long form: "MED SPA OWNERS: Here is how we help exceptional med
   spas attract exceptional patients." (The kill-list ban remains on "Here's the thing/truth/kicker.")
5. **Story-driven social proof:** one full named case story (Special K: 5-year slide, protein insight,
   "Keep the muscle, lose the fat," fastest-growing cereal — "the cereal never changed, the message did")
   beats a stat list. Number-one-brand claims stacked briefly around it. Guarantee arrives as the
   logical consequence of the mechanism.
6. **Aphorism openers work:** "Exceptional med spas need exceptional advertising." — tribal truth as
   line one, doubling as a headline.
7. **Structure note:** this reference package is 3 scripts / 5 headlines / 3 primary texts (3-5-3).

Let me explain why that happens and how you fix it.

Then we found the one thing it had more of than any other cereal. Protein. And when you diet without enough protein, you lose muscle instead of fat. So the message became simple. Keep the muscle, lose the fat.
