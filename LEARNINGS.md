# CUMULATIVE LEARNINGS (append-only — add a dated entry whenever a correction or new rule emerges)

## Core lessons (from the first 12 clients)

- **[NO AGENCY-BLAME / NO "MEDIA BUYER" BLAME]** — throwing stones rebounds since we ARE an agency.
  Refined rule: keep the contrast when it's the client's GENUINE wedge (operator-vs-agency, insider
  background); strip only gratuitous bashing. Frame pain inside the client's OWN world.
  (Learned via Chad 3-way comparison + Copy Chief.)
- **Lead scripts on a specific lived SCENE, not a metric.** Open on something the owner can picture
  (empty chair, trucks in the yard, dead DMs). Metric lives as proof underneath.
- **Tribal language + lived-scene pain on the FIRST pass**, not as revision rescue. List the niche's
  real vocabulary before writing.
- **Don't over-correct a rule into removing the client's edge.** (v2 Chad stripped his agency-wedge;
  Copy Chief restored it.)
- **Exact documented numbers beat rounded ones** when real and attributed ($1,542→$7,090, not "about $1,500").
- **Angle separation is a default, not a law** — two adjacent-territory scripts can run when both pains
  are strong AND that's where the client's proof lives.
- **Money-first in case studies** when the audience cares about income; pure-follower/reach wins last.
- **Founder-desire framing**: lead with what the founder WANTS, metric as proof underneath.
- **Soft/positioning offers**: vary the hero line across assets, keep risk-reversal + support line locked.
  Anchor strongest hero on ~6 assets, sprinkle 2-3 variations.
- **Audience splits are real** (Logan: brokers ≠ LOs). Address the reader by THEIR seat, with intentional
  pain-point splits and real terminology per audience.
- **Meta special-category caution** for financial/credit/housing niches: no published credit/income cutoffs,
  no "pre-qualified/approved," credit/income always about the LEADS never the reader.

## Copy Chief (Franbeau) patterns to preempt

Flags rushed first passes: generic copy lacking tribal language, robotic AI patterns ("Here's the thing"),
stray `**` in headers, wrong guarantee number, audience terms used interchangeably. Wants: intentional
audience splits by differing pain points, lead with predictability/strongest positioning, real industry
terminology.

## 2026-07-08 — Jack Lombardi session (roofing)

- **NO SPECIFIC MONTHS OR SEASONS in copy — standing rule for all clients.** Ads run nationwide across
  50 states with different weather and demand cycles. "Full in April, quiet by June" fails in half the
  country. Generalize: "some months... other months...", "the slow stretch." Weather EVENTS (a storm,
  hail hitting) are fine; calendar references are not.
- **When the client's proof is agency-flavored metrics, translate it.** CPL figures and "ad spend managed"
  make the ad smell like "another agency running ads." Use "$10M+ in marketing managed," lead case studies
  with lead increases, conversion lifts, and #1 rankings. Keep CPL for the sales call.
- **Business-name case studies are allowed with client approval** (Lafayette Fence & Gate, Oasis
  Landscaping). The first-names-only rule protects individual people, not businesses.
- **Named villains: factual, never degraded.** "Sites like Angi share that same homeowner with four other
  contractors" states the model; no trash talk.
- **Institutional credibility placement**: light stack in one script intro (I'm Jack + 2-3 proof points),
  full stack in the Social Proof primary text. Hold back jargon credentials (hosting counts, Semrush /
  Microsoft certs) — they read as agency wallpaper to a trades owner; sales-call material only.
- **Pull the "qualified" support line from the offer doc's own definition** (homeowner in service area,
  real roof issue, confirmed date and time) and keep it consistent across assets.
- **Sultanic hook banks adopted** (`HOOK_BANKS.md`): assign one structure per asset, no repeats in a
  package, adapt phrasing where the bank collides with the kill list ("But here's the truth" etc.).
- **Adjacent-trades proof works for a trades niche when framed one-directionally**: "we put local trades
  at #1, and today the same machine runs for roofing contractors." Never "we serve all home services."
- **Skill as final gate**: `final-package-review` runs on every package and after every revision round.
  Its two deliberate exceptions this session: client's own documented wedge line may keep a banned shape
  (flag it); numbered-list devices (Fact one/two/three, #1-#5) are exempt from the first-word rule.
- **The skill runs as a STANDALONE deliberate pass after every revision round, never inline.** An inline
  sweep during the Raquel revision missed 6 violations that the full six-pass run caught.

## 2026-07-08 — Raquel Bickford session (wedding vendors)

- **BANNED OPENER CONCEPT: "Picture this" / "Picture 90 days from now" / "Imagine..."** — retired
  permanently across all clients (user flagged it appearing in Raquel's and several recent packages).
  Future-state scripts open declaratively instead: "This is your business 90 days from now."
- Guarantee spines can be sharpened with the user mid-process: "20 new couples" became
  "20 new qualified couple inquiries" + support line, aligning the promise with what the agency
  actually controls (opportunities, not closings).
- Warm/insecure creative markets (wedding vendors): validation-first framing, no shame hooks,
  aesthetic-casual emoji ceiling (one ✨ max).
- When client proof is aesthetic-only (portfolio, no numbers): lead social proof with the client roster
  as a credits-roll list + one verbatim testimonial quote, founders' insider story underneath.

## 2026-07-08 — Ashley Elkins session (cosmetic dental)

- **DRAFT AT MAXIMUM TRIBAL DENSITY ON THE FIRST PASS.** Jack, Raquel, and Ashley all came back with
  "more tribal language" as the first revision. The bar: insider-only vocabulary the owner uses with
  staff, not just industry nouns. Dental example: morning huddle, doctor column vs hygiene column,
  case acceptance, wax-ups, production, treatment coordinator — not just "dentist" and "patients."
  Build the vocabulary list BEFORE writing and spend it.
- Empty credibility section → Insider Authority carries the social proof slot (Abe pattern). Provide the
  user a copy-paste ask for the client (named practice + one number, or a testimonial quote) with the
  upgrade path stated.
- Number conflicts can live inside the client's own sales scripts (40 in offer doc vs stale 20 in
  mechanism script) — flag both directions: fix the copy AND tell the client to fix the script.
